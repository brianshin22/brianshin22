# Brian Shin

Founder of [Silver Flight Group](https://silverflightgroup.com), building [Dubcap](https://dubcap.app), an AI tool that turns long-form video into short-form clips. Co-host of [Unlocalized](https://unlocalizedpod.com), a podcast about the founders and businesses in Korea that English-language media overlooks.

Previously a product manager at Waymo (simulation and evaluation for self-driving cars) and a software engineer turned PM at LG Electronics in Silicon Valley, where I launched the open-source [SVL Simulator](https://brianshin.me/projects/2021/08/21/svl-simulator.html), started LG's webOS robotics initiative, and represented LG on the ROS 2 and Autoware Foundation technical steering committees.

B.S. in Electrical Engineering, Harvard. M.Eng. in EECS, UC Berkeley. Based in Seoul.
